Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information RESEARCH_OPS processes and why it is required to run secure, high-fidelity training workflows.

Last updated: 30 Mar 2026 Scope: Platform operations

Information We Process

RESEARCH_OPS operates on a principle of minimal data persistence. We only process data essential to high-fidelity training simulations:

  • Identity Metadata: Usernames, hashed credentials, and system-provided email identifiers for session management.
  • Research Telemetry: Records of successful flag captures, completion timestamps, and lab enrollment states.
  • Operational Artifacts: Request logs and browser headers used exclusively for security audits and anti-abuse protocols.

Data Ownership and Portability

Research subjects retain ownership of their professional progress data. You may request a CSV export of your full performance telemetry through the Analyzer workspace if you hold elevated credentials, or through the Profile portal for standard users.

Third-Party Disclosure

We do not commercialize research telemetry. Data is never shared with external advertising entities. Disclosure is limited to institutional administrators and technical support leads for curriculum verification purposes.
