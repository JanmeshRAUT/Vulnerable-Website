Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information RESEARCH_OPS processes and why it is required to run secure, high-fidelity training workflows.

Last updated: 30 Mar 2026 Scope: Platform operations

Information We Process

  • Identity data such as username, role, and email address.
  • Session and authentication metadata needed to secure accounts.
  • Lab progress, solved units, timestamps, and flag submission records.
  • Request telemetry used for abuse detection and operational defense.

Why It Is Processed

Data is processed only to provide account access, validate training deliverables, maintain service integrity, and support educational reporting.

Use Limitations

RESEARCH_OPS does not use this data for ad targeting. Access is limited to platform administration, security operations, and approved academic workflows.
