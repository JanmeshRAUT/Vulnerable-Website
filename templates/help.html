Field Research FAQ

What is the Research Operations Platform? ▼

RESEARCH_OPS is a high-fidelity simulation environment designed for advanced web security exploitation. Each module represents a real-world vulnerability scenario where subjects must use clinical tools and methodology to extract research deliverables (flags).

The environment is designed to be black-box, meaning no hints or guidance are provided within the modules themselves. You must rely on your understanding of web protocols and security principles.

How do I submit a Research Deliverable (Flag)? ▼

Once you encounter a signal string formatted as FLAG{...}, follow these steps:

  1. Locate the flag icon at the bottom right of any lab page.
  2. Open the Research Deliverable Intake widget.
  3. Select the correct variation if applicable and paste your flag.
  4. Submit the deliverable to synchronize with the Command Center.

Successful submissions are recorded in your subject profile and verified by the command sensor array.

Are the flags unique to my identity? ▼

Yes. Every research deliverable is derived from your unique Subject Identity and the specific module. Sharing flags across users will fail verification.

What tools should I use for research? ▼

Recommended tooling includes intercepting proxies, browser developer tools, protocol analyzers, and carefully scoped automation.

  • Intercepting Proxies: Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP, or Caido
  • Analysis: Developer tools or Wireshark
  • Automation: Python, Go, ffuf, sqlmap where appropriate
Why is my account pending authorization? ▼

New subject enrollments require manual review to protect environment integrity. If the pending state persists beyond the normal review window, contact your research lead or administrator.

Can I reset a laboratory environment? ▼

Most modules are stateless and self-contained. If access is lost or a session becomes unstable, refresh the route or re-open the module from the curriculum index.
