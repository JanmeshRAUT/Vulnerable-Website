Field Research FAQ

What is the Research Operations Platform? ▼

RESEARCH_OPS is a high-fidelity simulation environment designed for advanced web security exploitation. Each module represents a real-world vulnerability scenario where subjects must use clinical tools and methodology to extract research deliverables (flags).

The environment is designed to be "black-box"—meaning no hints or guidance are provided within the modules themselves. You must rely on your understanding of web protocols and security principles.

How do I submit a Research Deliverable (Flag)? ▼

Once you encounter a signal string (formatted as FLAG{...}), follow these steps:

  1. Locate the red flag icon 🚩 at the bottom right of any lab page.
  2. Click it to open the **Research Deliverable Intake** widget.
  3. Select the correct variation (if applicable) and paste your flag.
  4. Click "Submit Research Deliverable" to synchronize with the Command Center.

Successful submissions are recorded in your subject profile and verified by the Command Center sensor array.

Are the flags unique to my identity? ▼

Yes. Every research deliverable is cryptographically derived from your unique Subject Identity (GUID) and the specific laboratory module. Sharing flags with other subjects will result in a synchronization failure and potential session termination.

What tools should I use for research? ▼

Subjects are encouraged to use industry-standard clinical equipment, including:

  • Intercepting Proxies: Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP, or Caido.
  • Protocol Analyzers: Wireshark or browser developer tools.
  • Automated Tooling: Custom scripts (Python/Go) or specialized scanners (ffuf, sqlmap) where permitted.

Remember: The platform simulates a hardened environment. Some payloads may require advanced encoding or bypass techniques.

Why is my account "Pending Authorization"? ▼

To maintain environment integrity, all new subject enrollments require manual vetting by the Operational Command Center. This process typically completes within 24 hours.

If your status remains PENDING after this window, contact your research lead or system administrator.

Can I reset a laboratory environment? ▼

Laboratory modules are stateless and self-contained. If you lose access or the environment becomes unstable, simply refresh the module URL or navigate back to the main registry to re-initialize your session.
